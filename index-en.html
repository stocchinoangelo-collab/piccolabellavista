The studio

A welcoming space, natural light and open views.

A studio of around 33 m² at Via Bellavista 14 in Pirri, for up to 2 guests. A double bed, kitchenette and private bathroom make it a comfortable place to settle in.

The balcony offers open views towards Cagliari and the sea. The studio is on the third floor with no lift: please consider the stairs when planning your stay.

  • Wi-Fi, TV and portable air conditioner.
  • Kitchenette, microwave and washing machine.
  • Free street parking nearby, subject to availability. No pets.

Location

Via Bellavista 14, Pirri, Cagliari.

Pirri is a Cagliari neighbourhood outside the historic centre. A practical location for reaching the airport, Brotzu, Oncologico and Microcitemico hospitals, and the roads towards Villasimius and southern Sardinia.

Directions

Central Cagliari and Poetto: about 15–20 minutes by car, depending on traffic. The airport is easily reached by car. Free parking nearby, with no reserved space.

FAQ

Before choosing your stay

How many guests can stay?

Up to 2 guests, with a double bed.

Are there stairs or a lift?

The studio is on the third floor with no lift.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed.

Where can I park?

Free parking nearby, subject to availability; there is no reserved space.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm, in person. If you arrive after 5:00 pm, let us know in advance and we will arrange it. Check-out by 11:00 am.

What amenities are available?

Wi-Fi, portable air conditioner, TV, kitchenette, microwave and washing machine.

When is my booking confirmed?

The request does not confirm the stay. You will receive availability, price, deposit, cancellation terms and any tourist tax in the proposal to accept.
